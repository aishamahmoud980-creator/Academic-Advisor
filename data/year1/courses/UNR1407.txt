Course Code: UNR1407
Course Name: Academic Writing
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 2
Credit Hours: 2
Prerequisites: UNR1403
Unlocks: None
Description:This course aims at enhancing students’ academic writing skills.
 The course provides both theoretical and practical bases for the students to master different types of
  skills needed for effective writing in an academic context. The course helps students to build on
   their writing strengths and learn how to remedy their weaknesses
 through constructive feedback.
