Course Code: CCS1001
Course Name: Discrete Structures
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 2
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: CCS1101
Unlocks: CAI3101 (requires CCS1001 AND CCS2401), CCS3402
Description:This course introduces the fundamental concepts of discrete mathematics used in computer science. 
It covers logic, propositions, predicates, set theory, relations, functions, mathematical proofs, counting techniques, 
and graph theory. Students develop logical thinking, problem-solving, and mathematical reasoning skills that are essential for understanding algorithms, 
data structures,and other advanced computer science topics.
