Course Code: EBA1203
Course Name: Calculus I
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 1
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: None (or EBA0201 for science students)
Unlocks: EBA1204
Description:This course introduces the fundamental concepts of differential calculus and their applications. 
It covers basic differentiation rules, derivatives of trigonometric, inverse trigonometric, logarithmic, exponential, 
and hyperbolic functions. Students also study implicit and parametric differentiation, partial differentiation, L’Hôpital’s rule, 
and Taylor and Maclaurin series expansions. The course develops mathematical and analytical skills through applications of derivatives, curve sketching, 
and optimization problems.
