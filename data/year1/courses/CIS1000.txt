Course Code: CIS1000
Course Name: Introduction to Information Systems
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 1
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: None
Unlocks: CSE2001 (requires CCS2303 AND CIS1000)
Description:This course introduces the fundamental concepts of Information Systems 
and their role in modern organizations. It covers the main components and functions of information systems, 
different types of information systems, business processes, and the use of information technology in management and 
decision-making. Students explore topics such as computer hardware and software, telecommunications and networks, 
database management systems, e-commerce, and enterprise information systems. The course helps students understand 
how information systems support business operations, improve organizational performance, and provide competitive advantages.
