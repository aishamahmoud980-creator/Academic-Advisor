Course Code: CCS3202
Course Name: Systems Programming
Department: Computer Science
Year: 3
Term: 5
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: CCS2303 AND CCS2103
Unlocks: CCS4306 (requires CCS3402 AND CCS3202); CCS4307 (requires CCS3202 AND CCS4306)
Description: This course introduces the concepts and techniques of system programming. The course covers the following topics: Machine Architecture; Machine Language; Assembly Language; Two Pass Assemblers; One Pass Assemblers; Macro Facilities; Conditional Macros; Macro Processors; Loaders; Linkers; Formal Languages; The Design of Translators (Compilers and Interpreters).
