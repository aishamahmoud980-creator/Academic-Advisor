Course Code: EBA1106
Course Name: Advanced Physics
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 2
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: Not specified in the provided source.
Unlocks: None
Description:This course builds on fundamental physics concepts and introduces students to more advanced principles of classical and modern physics. 
It explores topics related to mechanics, motion, forces, energy, momentum, rotational motion, oscillations, waves, electricity, and magnetism. 
Students develop analytical thinking, mathematical problem-solving, and the ability to apply physical principles to understand and solve scientific and engineering problems.
