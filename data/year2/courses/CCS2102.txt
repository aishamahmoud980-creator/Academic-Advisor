Course Code: CCS2102
Course Name: Digital Logic Design
Department: Common
Year: 2
Term: 3
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: CCS1101
Unlocks: CCS2103 (Prerequisites: CCS2102)
Description:This course aims to develop computer science skills in the design and analysis of digital logic circuits with applications to digital computing. It covers: Numbering systems, binary arithmetic and codes, logic gates, Boolean algebra and logic simplifications, Design and realization of combinational circuits, Functions of sequential logic circuits: Flip-Flops, analysis design and realization of counters.
