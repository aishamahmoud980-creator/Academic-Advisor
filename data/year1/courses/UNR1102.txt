Course Code: UNR1102
Course Name: Creativity and Innovation
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 1
Credit Hours: 2
Prerequisites: Not specified in the provided source.
Unlocks: None
Description:This course introduces the fundamental concepts of creativity and innovation 
and their importance in academic, professional, and personal development. 
It explores different aspects of creativity, cognitive abilities, and the environmental, social, personal, 
and organizational factors that influence creative thinking. Students learn various creativity techniques and problem-solving approaches, 
including mind mapping, Six Thinking Hats, SCAMPER, and bionics. The course also covers innovation, improvisation, invention, 
and the development of creative ideas through practical activities, projects, discussions, and presentations.
