Course Code: CCS3002
Course Name: Numerical Methods
Department: Computer Science
Year: 3
Term: 6
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: CCS1302 AND EBA2204
Unlocks: None
Description: This course provides an introduction to numerical methods and their applications to solve science and engineering problems. In addition, convergence and error analysis of numerical methods is covered. In addition, practical experience with implementing numerical methods and assessing resulting errors will be acquired through a number of programming assignments.
