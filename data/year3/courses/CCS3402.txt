Course Code: CCS3402
Course Name: Theory of Computation
Department: Computer Science
Year: 3
Term: 5
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: CCS1001
Unlocks: CCS4306 (requires CCS3402 AND CCS3202)
Description: This course introduces the fundamental mathematical models of computation. It covers the capabilities and limitations of computational models and their relationships with formal languages, including finite automata, regular languages, context-free grammars, pushdown automata, and Turing machines.
