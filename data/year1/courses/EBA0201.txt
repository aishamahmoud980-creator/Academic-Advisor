Course Code: EBA0201
Course Name: Precalculus
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 1
Credit Hours: 0
Prerequisites: None
Unlocks: EBA1203 (as an alternative prerequisite for science students)
Description:This course provides students with the essential mathematical foundations required for studying calculus and other advanced mathematics courses. 
It covers algebraic expressions, equations and inequalities, functions and their graphs, polynomial and rational functions, 
exponential and logarithmic functions, and trigonometric concepts. Students develop mathematical reasoning, analytical thinking, 
and problem-solving skills needed to understand more advanced mathematical topics, particularly calculus.
