Course Code: UNR1302
Course Name: Fundamentals of Business
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 1
Credit Hours: 2
Prerequisites: None
Unlocks: None
Description:This course introduces the fundamental concepts and principles of business and the role 
of business organizations in the modern economy. It covers the main business functions, 
including management, marketing, production, operations, human resource management, accounting, and finance. 
Students explore business ownership, entrepreneurship, organizational structures, business ethics, leadership, decision-making, 
and the global business environment. The course develops students' understanding of how businesses operate, manage resources, serve customers, 
and achieve organizational goals
