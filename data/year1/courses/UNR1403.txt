Course Code: UNR1403
Course Name: Academic English
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 1
Credit Hours: 2
Prerequisites: None (or GLA0001 for students who have not passed the English Proficiency Test)
Unlocks: UNR2101, UNR1407
Description:This course aims to develop students' four major language skills: reading, listening, 
speaking, and writing, through exposure to computing and information technology topics.
Students learn to understand technical English texts, communicate effectively using technical vocabulary, 
and apply different language functions. The course also helps students improve their ability to participate in discussions, 
understand lectures and interviews, take notes, and produce clear and well-organized academic writing.
