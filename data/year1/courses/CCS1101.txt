Course Code: CCS1101
Course Name: Introduction to Computing
Department: Common
Year: 1
Term: 1
Credit Hours: 3
Prerequisites: None
Unlocks: CCS1302, CCS1001, CCS2102
Description:This course provides an introduction to the fundamental concepts of computing and computer systems. 
It covers computer hardware and software, data representation, operating systems, computer networks, information technology, 
and basic computational concepts. Students gain an understanding of how computers work, how information is processed and stored,
and how computing technologies are used to solve real-world problems. The course builds a foundation for further studies in programming, 
computer science, and information technology.
