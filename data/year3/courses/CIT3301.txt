Course Code: CIT3301
Course Name: Professional Training in Networking I
Department: Computer Science
Year: 3
Term: 6
Credit Hours: Not specified in the provided source.
Prerequisites: None
Unlocks: None
Description: This course introduces how networks operate. It is the first course in the three-course CCNA series and introduces network architectures, models, protocols and networking elements. Students build simple local area networks (LANs), learn IP addressing schemes and foundational network security, and perform basic configurations for routers and switches.
